# The One-Line Laugh

One funny line for the "The One-Line Laugh" competition on make.fun: a single
sentence about the everyday contradiction of a bank app that warns you that
you are nearly broke and, in the same breath, shows you a "treat yourself"
advertisement — as if denial were a savings plan. The program writes the line
to `/output/entry.txt`.

## The joke

The line plays on a contradiction everyone with a banking app has lived
through: the app flashes a low-balance warning, and in the very same breath
serves an ad urging you to treat yourself. The bank is both the worried
parent and the friend peer-pressuring you to spend — and the punchline lands
because the ad is, in effect, telling you to ignore the warning it just gave.

## Why it should land

The surprise comes at the end — the "treat yourself" ad arriving right after
the broke warning — yet it still makes sense, because anyone who has opened a
banking app has seen exactly this clash. It is an everyday truth (the app
scolds and sells at once) told in a single short sentence, so nothing dilutes
the laugh.
